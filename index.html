🏸 Badminton Court Boundaries & Rules

In badminton, court boundaries change depending on whether you play Singles (1 vs 1) or Doubles (2 vs 2).

👤 Singles Match

"Long & Narrow"

  • Sideline: Inside line is used. The outer side tramlines are OUT.
  • Serve: Must land beyond the opponent's short service line and all the way back to the rear baseline.
  • Rally: Rear baseline is active for the whole rally.

👥 Doubles Match

"Wide & Short (Serve)"

  • Sideline: Outer sideline is IN across the entire court.
  • Serve: Short and wide! The back tramline is OUT during the serve.
  • Rally: Entire full court is in play after the serve.

⭐ Key Badminton Rules

  • Rally Point Scoring: Every rally earns 1 point for whoever wins it. First to 21 points wins the game.
  • Underhand Serve: The shuttlecock must be struck below waist level diagonally into the opponent's service box.
  • Even / Odd Serving Sides: When your score is even (0, 2, 4, ...), serve from the right side. When odd (1, 3, 5, ...), serve from the left side.
  • Faults: If the shuttle touches the net and fails to cross, lands outside the inner sideline, or touches a player's body/clothes, it is a fault.

🏸 Badminton Singles & Rules Guide

1. Singles vs Doubles Boundaries

In Badminton, the court lines change depending on whether you are playing singles (1 vs 1) or doubles (2 vs 2):

Singles: "Long and Narrow" • Sidelines: Inside line is the boundary (tramlines are OUT).
• Rear Line: Furthest back boundary line is always IN (both serve and rally).
Doubles: "Wide & Short (Serve)" • Sidelines: Full court width (tramlines are IN).
• Service: Back boundary is the inner long service line. Rally uses full back line.

2. Basic Badminton Rules (Primary 4 PE)

• Rally Point Scoring: Every rally won earns a point. First player to 21 points wins the set.

• Serving: The shuttle must be hit below waist level and diagonally across the net past the opponent's short service line.

• Even/Odd Service Rule: When your score is even (0, 2, 4...), serve from the right service court. When odd (1, 3, 5...), serve from the left court.

• Common Faults: Hitting the shuttle into the net, shuttle landing out of boundary lines, touching the net with your racket or body, or hitting the shuttle twice before it crosses the net.

3. Singles Strategy Tips

🏸 Make them run: Place the shuttle diagonally opposite from where the opponent is standing (Four Corners strategy).

🎯 Avoid the reach zone: If you hit right back to the opponent, they can smash or kill the shuttlecock without running.
